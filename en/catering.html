Traiteur Tahraoui, in collaboration with Or Matrix: generous cuisine for all your events, and a catering and provisioning service for your stopovers. Our offer

Delicious cuisine, delivered at every stopover

  • Traiteur: buffets, cocktails, weddings, engagement parties, seminars and religious celebrations
  • Mechoui (whole roast lamb) on order
  • Airline catering: meal trays and supplies for airlines and aircraft
  • Ship provisioning and supply of ports
  • Supply of airports and stopover areas
  • Hotels, restaurants and institutions: meals and produce delivered to your needs
  • Full meals, lunch boxes and individual trays for companies, construction sites and training sessions
  • From small groups to several hundred covers
  • Delivery by refrigerated truck, organised and punctual, at the agreed time
  • Freshness, hygiene and food safety guaranteed
